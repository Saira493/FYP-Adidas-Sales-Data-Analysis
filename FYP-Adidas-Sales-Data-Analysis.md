## Abstract
The purpose of this research is to identify ways in which techniques used in organizational data
analysis and business modeling can be used to make the sales forecast for Adidas in the US.
Utilizing a comprehensive dataset comprising 9,653 sales records across various dimensions, the
research employs a dual approach: For sales forecasting it will use time series analysis and on the
other hand for the customer segments it will use clustering. The first aim is, therefore, to assess
the accuracy of many time series forecasting methodologies and the second aim is to perform
clustering of the customers’ buying behavior thoroughly. Therefore, simultaneously using both
these methodologies in the study, it is possible to provide a comprehensive view of the Adidas
sales characteristics and contribute with some strategic inferences about the inventory control, as
well as regarding the kind of marketing campaigns that should be launched within the context of
sports apparel industry. The papers contribute to the existing literature into bringing awareness on
the applicability of Data Science in retail analytics, with a focus on sales high-dimensional data.
